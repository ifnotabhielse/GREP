How it works

From sign-up to race day.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us your goal

    Your next target, your current pace or training volume, and the slots you can train in.

  2. Step 2

    Get matched

    We place you in a cohort of 3 to 6 people at your pace, near you, on a schedule that works for everyone.

  3. Step 3

    Train to race day

    A fixed meeting point, a shared weekly plan and a group chat. People expect you there, so you show up.

After race day, set your next goal and we match you again.

Questions

Good to know.

What if there's no cohort for my goal yet?

Sign up anyway. We keep your details and open a cohort once enough people at your pace, in your area, want the same thing.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. A rough idea of your pace or weekly training is enough. If you use Apple Watch, Garmin, WHOOP or Strava, your numbers make the match more accurate.

How do cohorts meet?

In person, at a fixed meeting point, on the agreed days. Each cohort also gets a WhatsApp group and a shared weekly plan.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older. The first cohorts are in Hyderabad. Other cities open when enough people there sign up.
